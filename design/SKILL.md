# Design

This is how design work gets done, from an idea to a page the owner is happy with on their own phone. It comes from a home page that took more than a week and about 30 rounds. Most of that time went on three things:
- the business under the page kept changing;
- the reviewers read every word, when real buyers skim;
- facts only the owner had were asked for late.

Doing the steps below in order avoids all three.

## The flow

| Step | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Settle the business** before any design: a real customer story, a fact sheet the owner signs, a panel by segment plus the end customer, pricing tested with the cost per unit, and how the design reaches the real product | [ITERATE.md](ITERATE.md) section A, [business fact sheet](references/business-fact-sheet.md), [pricing interview](references/pricing-interview.md) |
| 2 | **Interview the buyers** about risk, then separately about value; send the owner one batch of fact questions, each with an example | [ITERATE.md](ITERATE.md) section 2, [risk](references/interview-risk.md), [value](references/interview-value.md) |
| 3 | **Write the brief:** the idea and the feeling first, 2–3 references, real product and data, one direction deep, only factual constraints | [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md) sections 1–3 |
| 4 | **Wide first round** when the owner wants to choose: a world per option, plans first, self-critique, all options side by side | [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md) section 4 |
| 5 | **Review the round:** customer reviews per group (skimming), two blind glance tests, a visual critique, one combined score; then a fix round | [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md) section 5, [skim review](references/skim-review-brief.md) |
| 6 | **Take the chosen one to done:** one page and one link, one builder resumed every round, the owner's page comments read every round, the panel average (never one score), stop when it plateaus | [ITERATE.md](ITERATE.md) sections 0–7, [builder brief](references/builder-round-brief.md), [pre-publish scan](references/pre-publish-scan.md) |
| 7 | **Words:** headlines by a two-round slogan test; small wording through page comments; every claim traced to the fact sheet or the PRD | [ITERATE.md](ITERATE.md) section 3, [slogan test](references/slogan-test.md) |

## The rules that matter most

- **No design before the business is settled.** If the product, the price or the promises are still moving, the page will be redesigned when they move.
- **Real buyers skim.** Reviewers get about a minute, headlines and pictures first. A home page holds 1,200 words at most, and everything else goes on a details page.
- **The owner's comments outrank the panel**, and they don't notify you. Read every thread every round.
- **Judge on the panel average.** One reviewer swings by up to 8 points between identical rounds.
- **A plateau means facts, not wording.** Report what's missing and the points each item is worth, then stop.
- **Honesty:** no invented numbers, people, quotes or results. Example values are labelled. Every claim has a source.
- **Brand identity** (logo, brand colour) goes to a human identity designer. AI drafts ideas for them.
- **Heavy builds run on a server,** not on the laptop doing the design loop.
