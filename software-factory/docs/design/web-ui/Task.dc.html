What it is

The board shows every task as a card in its lane: Ready, In progress, In review, Blocked and Done today. Swim lanes group the cards by epic or by agent. A card shows who holds it, its pull request and whether it needs you.

It reads from the cache, so it opens without waiting on GitHub, and says how old its data is.
