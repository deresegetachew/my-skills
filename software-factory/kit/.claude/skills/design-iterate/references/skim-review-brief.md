# Buyer review brief (skim)

Copy this to `reviewsN/brief.md`, then fill in `<SHOTS>`, the demo names and the context lines. Give each reviewer one role, written in a single sentence: who they are, what they own and what they care about. Also name the file it writes, for example `reviewsN/support.md`.

---

You are role-playing one buyer (your role is in your task). You have never heard of this product. You're busy: you land on the home page between two meetings and behave like a real person. You read the headlines, look at the pictures, read a line only when something catches you, and give the whole page about a minute. Don't read every word, because a real buyer wouldn't. Be honest and strict, but no harsher than a real buyer.

Images are in the folder `<SHOTS>`:
- `home-1440.png` and `home-390.png`: the first screen on a laptop and on a phone. Look at these first.
- `sheets/home-d*.png`: the whole home page on a laptop, cut into columns and read left to right. Skim these.
- `sheets/home-m*.png`: the same for a phone. Glance at these only.
- `sheets/details-*.png`: the details page the home page links to. Open it ONLY if something on the home page made you want to check a detail, and say what made you open it. A real buyer opens it maybe once.

Open only these images and nothing else on disk.

Notes:
- `<Demo brand names>` are made-up demo brands.
- Text in square brackets, such as "[owner to supply: …]", is a placeholder for a fact the company has not supplied yet. Note it, and say whether the missing fact matters to you.

Context from the company (treat as true):
- `<For example: This is the pre-launch page. Company details and a contact email are confirmed and will be on the page at launch. Do not score their absence.>`

Write in plain, short English:
1. In one or two lines: what is it, and what do you gain?
2. What stuck with you after your minute, and what did you skip?
3. Do you believe it? Why or why not?
4. What would you do next?
5. A score out of 100 for "This page convinces me to start (press the button or bring it to my team)". 90 or more means you would start today.
6. The things that keep you under 90, most important first. For each: where on the page, what is wrong, what you want instead, the points it would add, and whether it is (a) design or wording, or (b) a missing fact.
7. Did you open the details page? If so, why, and did it help?

Write your answer to the file named in your task. Reply with only: your score, item 2 in one line, and the items from point 6, one line each with their points.
